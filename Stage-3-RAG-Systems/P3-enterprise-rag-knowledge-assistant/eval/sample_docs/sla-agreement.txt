Service Level Agreement
Enterprise Edition | Version 2.2 | Effective Date: 2024-01-01

1. OVERVIEW

This Service Level Agreement (SLA) defines the uptime commitments, response time
guarantees, escalation procedures, and compensation structures for enterprise customers.
This agreement is incorporated by reference into the Master Services Agreement.

2. UPTIME COMMITMENTS

The platform commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for Standard tier customers and 99.95%
for Premium and Mission Critical tier customers, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.
Uptime is calculated monthly as: (total minutes - downtime minutes) / total minutes.

3. INCIDENT RESPONSE TIME COMMITMENTS

P1 - Critical: Initial response within 15 minutes, status update every 30 minutes.
P2 - High: Initial response within 1 hour, status update every 2 hours.
P3 - Medium: Initial response within 4 hours, next business day resolution target.
P4 - Low: Response within 2 business days.

4. ESCALATION THRESHOLDS

4.1 Executive Escalation
Executive escalation is triggered when P1 incidents exceed 4 hours or P2 incidents
exceed 24 hours. Upon triggering executive escalation, the account VP and customer
executive sponsor are automatically notified via the escalation management system.

Executive escalation does not replace standard incident response procedures but adds
a parallel executive communication track and may unlock additional engineering resources.

4.2 Escalation Contacts
All escalation contacts are maintained in the customer account profile and must be
reviewed and updated at least quarterly.

5. SERVICE CREDITS AND COMPENSATION

5.1 Credit Calculation
Compensation starts at 10% service credit for SLA breach and increases to 30% for
breaches exceeding 3x the SLA threshold. Credits are calculated as a percentage of
the monthly subscription fee for the affected service.

Specifically:
- SLA breach (1x threshold): 10% service credit
- Breach exceeding 2x threshold: 20% service credit
- Breach exceeding 3x threshold: 30% service credit
- Breach exceeding 5x threshold: 50% service credit (Mission Critical tier only)

5.2 Credit Request Process
Service credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident. Credits are applied
to the next invoice cycle and are not redeemable for cash. Credits cannot exceed the
total monthly subscription value.

5.3 Exclusions
SLA commitments do not apply during scheduled maintenance, customer-caused outages,
force majeure events, or third-party service failures outside the platform's control.

6. REVIEW AND AMENDMENT

This SLA is reviewed annually. Customers will be notified 90 days before any material
changes to the SLA terms.
