HR Records Access Policy
Classification: CONFIDENTIAL | Version 2.0 | Maintained by: Human Resources & Security

1. PURPOSE

This policy governs access to confidential Human Resources records and personnel files.
HR records contain sensitive personal information and are subject to strict access
controls under applicable privacy regulations.

2. SCOPE

This policy applies to all HR records including but not limited to: personnel files,
performance reviews, disciplinary records, compensation information, benefits data,
and medical accommodation requests.

3. APPROVAL AUTHORITY

Access to confidential HR records can only be approved by the CHRO or designated
HR security officers. Requests must be submitted with a business justification and
are reviewed within 48 hours.

Designated HR security officers are appointed by the CHRO and maintain a current
list in the HR system. Employees can view the list of designated HR security officers
through the HR portal.

3.1 Delegation of Approval
The CHRO may delegate approval authority to designated HR security officers for
standard access requests. High-sensitivity requests (including executive compensation
data and disciplinary records involving senior management) require CHRO approval
and cannot be delegated.

4. REQUEST PROCESS

4.1 Submitting an Access Request
Access requests must be submitted through the HR portal using the Confidential
Records Access Request form. Requests must include:
- Requester name and employee ID
- Specific records required (name of employee and record type)
- Business justification for access
- Duration of access required (temporary vs. ongoing)
- Manager co-authorization signature

4.2 Review Process
The CHRO or designated HR security officer reviews the request and business
justification. Requests are reviewed within 48 hours. Incomplete requests are
returned to the requester for additional information.

Approved access is provisioned within 4 business hours of approval.
Denied requests include a written explanation of the denial reason.

5. ACCESS CONTROLS

All access to HR records is logged with full audit trails. Access sessions are
limited to 60 minutes before requiring re-authentication. Printing HR records
requires separate approval and is restricted to designated secure print stations.

6. DATA HANDLING

HR records may not be copied, forwarded, or shared with any individual who has not
been specifically granted access. Records must be viewed only in approved secure
environments. Remote access to HR records requires VPN and compliant device certification.

7. RETENTION AND DISPOSAL

HR records are retained in accordance with the Data Governance Policy and applicable
employment law requirements. Access to records scheduled for disposal is restricted
to the disposal approval period only.
