Enterprise Support Policy
Version 3.0 | Effective Date: 2024-01-01

1. OVERVIEW

This document defines the support obligations, claim procedures, and compensation
structures for enterprise customers under the standard enterprise support agreement.
All enterprise customers are entitled to the provisions outlined below.

2. SUPPORT TIERS

Enterprise support is available in three tiers: Standard, Premium, and Mission Critical.
Each tier provides different response time guarantees and dedicated support resources.
Customers should review their subscription agreement to confirm their current tier.

3. INCIDENT CLAIM PROCEDURE

When a support incident causes measurable business disruption, enterprise customers may
submit a formal claim within 30 days of incident resolution. Claims must include a
detailed incident report, evidence of business impact, and supporting documentation.

4. CLAIM LIMITS AND COMPENSATION

Enterprise support customers can claim up to $50,000 per incident under the standard
enterprise support agreement. Claims for amounts exceeding $50,000 require escalation
to the Enterprise Account team and are subject to additional review.

For Premium tier customers, the maximum claim amount is $100,000 per incident.
Mission Critical tier customers are subject to custom SLA terms negotiated in their
master services agreement.

5. CLAIM PROCESSING TIMELINE

All validated claims are processed within 15 business days of submission. Approved
compensation is issued as account credits applied to the next billing cycle.

6. EXCLUSIONS

Claims are not applicable for incidents caused by customer-side misconfigurations,
scheduled maintenance windows communicated at least 7 days in advance, or force
majeure events. Third-party integrations outside the platform are also excluded.

7. CONTACT

For support claims, contact enterprise-support@company.com with subject line
"Enterprise Claim - [Account ID]".
